Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tips i tjänst ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use our App:

  • Account Information: Username and password when you create an account.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Device tokens for sending push notifications, if you opt in.
  • Usage Data: Your betting selections and participation in tipping rounds.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality.
  • Send push notifications about new rounds, game deadlines, and live updates (if enabled).
  • Display leaderboards and standings among participants.
  • Improve the App experience.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on servers hosted in Europe (Microsoft Azure, West Europe region). We retain your data for as long as your account is active.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Service Providers: We use Expo (push notifications) and Microsoft Azure (hosting) to operate the App.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights.

Push Notifications

You can opt in or out of push notifications at any time through the App's settings. We offer notifications for:

  • New betting rounds available
  • Game stop reminders
  • Live match updates

Third-Party Services

The App uses data from Svenska Spel's public API to display Stryktipset information. We do not share your personal data with Svenska Spel.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time.

Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your personal information, including secure authentication and encrypted data transmission (HTTPS).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the App.
